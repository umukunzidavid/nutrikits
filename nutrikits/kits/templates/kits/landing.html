Please read: using NutriKits safely

NutriKits is a cooking and shopping helper. It is not a medical service. We don't diagnose conditions, prescribe treatment or replace your doctor, nurse or dietitian.

🩺

Your doctor's advice comes first

If a health professional has given you a specific plan (foods to avoid, portion sizes, calorie targets, medicines), follow that plan. If it differs from anything on this site, your doctor is right and the site is not.

🏷️

Health tags are not prescriptions

A tag such as "Diabetes-friendly" or "Hypertension" means the meal follows general healthy-eating ideas for that goal. It does not mean the meal is right for you, and the nutrition note on each meal is general information, not personal medical advice.

🚫

Don't pick a focus to treat yourself

Choose a health focus only if a health professional recommended it. Don't use this site to decide whether you have a condition, and never change or stop medicines (including insulin) because of a meal.

🤰

Take extra care if you…

are pregnant or breastfeeding, are cooking for a baby or a child with a medical condition, have kidney or heart disease, or take several medicines (some foods affect certain medicines). Ask your clinic before changing what you eat.

🥜

Allergies and intolerances

The "ingredients to avoid" list hides meals that contain the ingredient names you type in. It can't catch hidden ingredients, spelling differences or cross-contamination. For a serious allergy, always check every ingredient yourself.

🔢

Numbers are estimates

Calories, quantities, prep times and prices are approximate. Final delivery fees and totals are shown before you order.

Feeling unwell? Don't wait for a meal to fix it. Contact your nearest health centre or hospital, or your local emergency service, especially for severe symptoms such as chest pain, difficulty breathing, fainting, or very high or low blood sugar.
